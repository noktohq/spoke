Spoke — Privacy Policy

Effective: September 2026

Spoke is made by Nokto (nokto.no). We collect as little as possible.

Your data stays on your device

Bikes and service entries you log are stored locally on your phone. They are never uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes the log, unless you exported it yourself first.

Price negotiation

When you haggle for Spoke+, the app sends your offers (amounts only) to our negotiation server so the AI dealer can respond. Negotiation sessions are held briefly in server memory, are not tied to your identity, and are not stored.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Google Play and by RevenueCat, our billing infrastructure provider. RevenueCat receives purchase information (such as a random app user ID and transaction details) to manage your entitlements. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy and Google Play's terms. We do not receive your payment card details.

No ads, no tracking

Spoke contains no advertising and no third-party analytics.
