## Full description (4000 tegn maks)

Spoke is a clean, fast service log for your bikes.

Keep every bike's history in one place: services, part swaps, chain and brake
wear, and what it all cost. No account, no ads, no tracking — your log lives
on your phone.

THE WORLD'S FIRST NEGOTIABLE SUBSCRIPTION

Spoke+ (unlimited bikes and full service-history export) has no fixed price.
Instead, you haggle with our AI bike dealer. Make an offer. Get a counter.
Push your luck. The price you agree on is the price you pay — locked for 48
hours. Lazy offers close high; sharp negotiators ride away with the best
deal. The dealer has a hard floor it can never be talked below, so every
outcome is one the merchant already approved.

FEATURES

• Garage: all your bikes in one place
• Service log per bike: what, when, and what it cost
• Spoke+: unlimited bikes and CSV export of your full history
• The negotiable paywall: your price is your negotiation

Built by Nokto (nokto.no). The negotiation engine is the same one that powers
our AI-to-AI price haggling on real e-commerce stores.
